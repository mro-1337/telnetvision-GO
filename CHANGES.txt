TELNETVISION FOR WINDOWS - WHAT'S NEW
=====================================

VERSION 1.5.0
-------------

  Jumping around in a video (camera PC)
    - J: type a time to go to (1:05:00, 45:00, 90 for minutes, 1h30m,
      or +10 / -5 minutes from here). Left / Right arrow: back / ahead
      5 minutes (SEEK_STEP). Picture, sound and subtitles all carry on
      from there; viewers stay connected.
    - The status line shows where the video is ("12:34 / 1:45:00").

  Re-encoding movies this PC can't decode fast enough (REENCODE=1)
    - When a movie file plays slower than FPS for several seconds,
      ffmpeg makes a lighter copy (H.264, 720 pixels wide, AAC sound)
      from the exact place it had reached, and the movie carries on from
      there once enough of it is made. Viewers see a please-wait notice
      with how far along it is.
    - The copy is a temporary file, deleted when the movie ends or is
      skipped, and when telnetvision closes (left-over copies are
      cleared at the next start).
    - New settings REENCODE, REENCODE_WIDTH, REENCODE_CRF,
      REENCODE_PRESET, REENCODE_FOLDER.

  The playlist for viewers (L)
    - A viewer who is the only one watching presses L for a lightbar
      list of the camera PC's playlist: arrow keys move, Enter plays
      the highlighted entry now, Space tags it to play next (in the
      order tagged). No typing. It closes if someone else starts
      watching. The web page has a Playlist button with on-screen
      buttons for touch screens.
    - N on the camera PC plays the tagged entries first.

  Fixes
    - Option 8 (Auto detail) in the viewers' S menu didn't respond to
      the 8 key.
    - The Win64 README's title said "(32-bit)".
    - Closing the camera window now stops cleanly (ffmpeg, temporary
      files), as Q does.


VERSION 1.4.1
-------------

  Web page
    - Full screen really fills the screen now. Before, the picture stayed
      at its page size (at most 960x575) in the top of the screen; now
      it's scaled up to the largest size that fits, centred on black.
    - Double-click the picture to go full screen and back; an "Exit full
      screen" button sits in the corner (Esc works too).
    - Phones that can't do full screen for web pages (iPhone) fill the
      browser window instead.


VERSION 1.4.0
-------------

  JPEG XL detail
    - JPEG XL viewers pick their detail with S, option 7: High
      (320x192), Medium (240x144) or Low (160x96, about a third of
      High's data). The camera PC makes each level only while someone
      watches at it. New camera PC settings JXL_MEDIUM_WIDTH,
      JXL_MEDIUM_QUALITY, JXL_LOW_WIDTH, JXL_LOW_QUALITY; new
      tvserver.ini setting jxl_detail (where viewers start).
    - Option 7 is now "Detail", for sixel and JPEG XL alike (each keeps
      its own setting).

  Auto detail
    - When a sixel or JPEG XL viewer's connection can't keep up, their
      detail goes down a step by itself (High -> Medium -> Low), with a
      notice along the top, and back up after 30 seconds of keeping up.
      It never goes above what the viewer picked. S, option 8 turns it
      off for a viewer; auto_detail = off in tvserver.ini for everyone.
    - In a test at 0.3 Mbit/s, a JPEG XL viewer went from 4.5 to 15
      frames a second.

  Works with 1.3.0: a 1.3.0 camera PC makes High only (the menu says
  so), and a 1.3.0 tvserver asks a 1.4.0 camera PC for High only.


VERSION 1.3.0
-------------

  JPEG XL pictures
    - New Colors choice "JPEG XL" for terminals that can draw JPEG XL
      (SyncTERM 1.10+ and terminals that copy its commands): real
      pixels, full colour, sharper than sixel at about half the data.
    - The server asks each terminal whether it can draw JPEG XL and
      only offers the choice to those that say yes.
    - The camera PC makes the pictures once for every JPEG XL viewer,
      only while somebody watches in JPEG XL. Needs an ffmpeg with
      libjxl; see the next point.
    - If the camera PC's ffmpeg can't make JPEG XL, telnetvision.exe
      downloads one that can, once (about 90 MB, into the ffmpeg-jxl
      folder next to it), and uses it for the JPEG XL pictures.
      JXL_DOWNLOAD=0 turns that off.
    - The web page shows JPEG XL too: with the browser's own decoder
      when it has one, else with libjxl as WebAssembly (jSquash).
    - New camera PC settings JXL_WIDTH (320), JXL_QUALITY (75) and
      JXL_DOWNLOAD (1); new tvserver.ini setting  jxl = on.


VERSION 1.2.0
-------------

Camera PC (telnetvision.exe)

  Screens
    - SOURCE=screen1, screen2, ... sends one monitor (1 is the main one,
      then left to right). SOURCE=screen still sends all of them.
    - SOURCE=window:<part of a title> sends one window.
    - SOURCE=screen:X,Y,WxH sends any area of the desktop.
    - telnetvision.exe --list-devices lists the screens with their sizes
      and positions, and --setup offers each one.

  Music
    - Songs (mp3, flac, ogg, opus, m4a, wav, wma, ...), folders of music,
      playlists of songs and internet radio play with a music visualizer
      as the picture: 12 looks in the spirit of Milkdrop that move with
      the bass, mids, treble and beats, blending into a new look now and
      then. V switches to the next look.
    - Songs' artist and title tags show in viewers' title bar.
    - SOURCE=visualizer shows the visualizer for the microphone or
      line-in (AUDIO_SOURCE).
    - New settings: VISUALIZER (auto, or the look to start on) and
      VIZ_SECONDS (how often it changes; 0 = only with V).

  YouTube
    - Each video is downloaded completely before it plays, and the next
      one downloads while it plays (one at a time), so the network can't
      interrupt it. Live streams are still streamed. New setting:
      YOUTUBE_PREFETCH (1 = on, the default).
    - Works with YouTube's separate picture and sound streams (fixes
      "Requested format is not available").
    - Streamed sound is fetched in small pieces with retries, so it no
      longer cuts out partway ("Error during demuxing: I/O error"); if it
      does stop, playback picks up again from the same spot.
    - A link to a video inside a playlist (watch?v=...&list=...) plays
      the whole playlist, starting from that video.
    - Clearer advice when YouTube refuses (update yt-dlp, install Deno).

  Sixel
    - Sixel viewers now get the camera PC's sharp picture every frame.
      Before, a timing slip meant most frames fell back to the ordinary
      80-wide picture, so High and Medium detail often looked blocky.
      For sharper sixel still, set SIXEL_WIDTH=320.

  Keyboard
    - Paste with Ctrl+V, Shift+Insert or a right-click: what you copied
      (a YouTube link, a path) goes on the Play line, or into the line
      you're typing.

Server (tvserver.exe), BBS door (door.exe) and relay (service.exe)

  - No changes apart from the version number.
