TELNETVISION FOR WINDOWS - WHAT'S NEW
=====================================

VERSION 1.3.0
-------------

  JPEG XL pictures
    - New Colors choice "JPEG XL" for terminals that can draw JPEG XL
      (SyncTERM 1.10+ and terminals that copy its commands): real
      pixels, full colour, sharper than sixel at about half the data.
    - The server asks each terminal whether it can draw JPEG XL and
      only offers the choice to those that say yes.
    - The camera PC makes the pictures once for every JPEG XL viewer,
      only while somebody watches in JPEG XL. Needs an ffmpeg with
      libjxl; see the next point.
    - If the camera PC's ffmpeg can't make JPEG XL, telnetvision.exe
      downloads one that can, once (about 90 MB, into the ffmpeg-jxl
      folder next to it), and uses it for the JPEG XL pictures.
      JXL_DOWNLOAD=0 turns that off.
    - The web page shows JPEG XL too: with the browser's own decoder
      when it has one, else with libjxl as WebAssembly (jSquash).
    - New camera PC settings JXL_WIDTH (320), JXL_QUALITY (75) and
      JXL_DOWNLOAD (1); new tvserver.ini setting  jxl = on.


VERSION 1.2.0
-------------

Camera PC (telnetvision.exe)

  Screens
    - SOURCE=screen1, screen2, ... sends one monitor (1 is the main one,
      then left to right). SOURCE=screen still sends all of them.
    - SOURCE=window:<part of a title> sends one window.
    - SOURCE=screen:X,Y,WxH sends any area of the desktop.
    - telnetvision.exe --list-devices lists the screens with their sizes
      and positions, and --setup offers each one.

  Music
    - Songs (mp3, flac, ogg, opus, m4a, wav, wma, ...), folders of music,
      playlists of songs and internet radio play with a music visualizer
      as the picture: 12 looks in the spirit of Milkdrop that move with
      the bass, mids, treble and beats, blending into a new look now and
      then. V switches to the next look.
    - Songs' artist and title tags show in viewers' title bar.
    - SOURCE=visualizer shows the visualizer for the microphone or
      line-in (AUDIO_SOURCE).
    - New settings: VISUALIZER (auto, or the look to start on) and
      VIZ_SECONDS (how often it changes; 0 = only with V).

  YouTube
    - Each video is downloaded completely before it plays, and the next
      one downloads while it plays (one at a time), so the network can't
      interrupt it. Live streams are still streamed. New setting:
      YOUTUBE_PREFETCH (1 = on, the default).
    - Works with YouTube's separate picture and sound streams (fixes
      "Requested format is not available").
    - Streamed sound is fetched in small pieces with retries, so it no
      longer cuts out partway ("Error during demuxing: I/O error"); if it
      does stop, playback picks up again from the same spot.
    - A link to a video inside a playlist (watch?v=...&list=...) plays
      the whole playlist, starting from that video.
    - Clearer advice when YouTube refuses (update yt-dlp, install Deno).

  Sixel
    - Sixel viewers now get the camera PC's sharp picture every frame.
      Before, a timing slip meant most frames fell back to the ordinary
      80-wide picture, so High and Medium detail often looked blocky.
      For sharper sixel still, set SIXEL_WIDTH=320.

  Keyboard
    - Paste with Ctrl+V, Shift+Insert or a right-click: what you copied
      (a YouTube link, a path) goes on the Play line, or into the line
      you're typing.

Server (tvserver.exe), BBS door (door.exe) and relay (service.exe)

  - No changes apart from the version number.
